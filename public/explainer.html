05

A shared clock keeps the world coherent.

Every actor reads the same previous checkpoint. Every week ends with a new immutable frame.

01 / OBSERVEFreeze the previous stateRecent history, shared by all actors.
02 / CHOOSESelect bounded actionsAI review or deterministic rules.
03 / ACTIVATEReceive due arrivalsPending policies become effective.
04 / COMMITApply feasible choicesSpend budgets; dispatch shipments.
05 / SETTLEProduce and pay billsInventory, wages, prices, savings.
06 / RECORDAppend the checkpointState, decisions, queues, pipeline.
